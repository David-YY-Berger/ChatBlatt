But for your own life-blood I will require a reckoning: I will require it of every beast; of humankind, too, will I require a reckoning for human life, of everyone for each other! Whoever sheds human blood, By human [hands] shall that ones blood be shed; For in the image of God Was humankind made. Be fertile, then, and increase; abound on the earth and increase on it. I will maintain My covenant with you: never again shall all flesh be cut off by the waters of a flood, and never again shall there be a flood to destroy the earth.

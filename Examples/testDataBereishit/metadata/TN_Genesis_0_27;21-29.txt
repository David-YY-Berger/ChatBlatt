Isaac said to Jacob, Come closer that I may feel you, my son-whether you are really my son Esau or not. So Jacob drew close to his father Isaac, who felt him and wondered. The voice is the voice of Jacob, yet the hands are the hands of Esau. He did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like those of his brother Esau; and so he blessed him. He asked, Are you really my son Esau? And when he said, I am, he said, Serve me and let me eat of my sons game that I may give you my innermost blessing.

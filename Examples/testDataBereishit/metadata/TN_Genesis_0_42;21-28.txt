That is why this distress has come upon us. Then Reuben spoke up and said to them, Did I not tell you, Do no wrong to the boy? But you paid no heed. Now comes the reckoning for his blood. So they loaded their asses with the rations and departed from there. As one of them was opening his sack to give feed to his ass at the night encampment, he saw his money right there at the mouth of his bag. And he said to his brothers, My money has been returned!

Isaac dug anew the wells which had been dug in the days of his father Abraham and which the Philistines had stopped up after Abrahams death; and he gave them the same names that his father had given them. But when Isaacs servants, digging in the wadi, found there a well of spring water, the herdsmen of Gerar quarreled with Isaacs herdsmen, saying, The water is ours.
